The MIMIC-IV-ED vitalsign table stores the vital signs that nurses document for patients admitted to the emergency department, which are routinely taken every 1 to 4 hours. It has 1,564,610 rows and links to the edstays table on stay_id. The table has the columns subject_id, stay_id, charttime, temperature, heartrate, resprate, o2sat, sbp, dbp, rhythm and pain. The charttime column is the time at which the vital signs were charted.

The numeric entries in the vitalsign table were originally stored as free-text, so the columns required deidentification. Free-text entries which could not be converted trivially were removed. Missing data in the numeric columns indicates either deidentified data or no data recorded, but for the most part missing data means that no information was documented. In version 2.1, 564968 rows were missing temperature, of which 11048 (about 2 percent) had a free-text value deleted during deidentification.

In the vitalsign table, temperature is the patient's temperature in degrees Fahrenheit, and some temperatures may be incorrectly documented as Celsius. The heartrate column is the heart rate in beats per minute and the resprate column is the respiratory rate in breaths per minute. The o2sat column is the oxygen saturation measured as a percentage. The sbp and dbp columns are the systolic and diastolic blood pressure measured in millimetres of mercury (mmHg).

The rhythm column of the vitalsign table is the patient's heart rhythm. The pain column is the patient's self-reported level of pain on a scale from 0-10, documented as free-text, so it may contain non-numeric entries. In version 2.1, rows missing data were 69710 for heart rate, 89393 for respiratory rate, 135836 for oxygen saturation and 81256 for both systolic and diastolic blood pressure.

The MIMIC-IV-ED edstays table is the primary tracking table for emergency department visits. It provides the time the patient entered the emergency department and the time they left the emergency department. It has 425,087 rows and the columns subject_id, hadm_id, stay_id, intime, outtime, gender, race, arrival_transport and disposition. The stay_id identifies a single emergency department stay for a single patient.

If the patient was admitted to the hospital after their ED stay, the hadm_id column contains the hospital identifier, which ranges from 2000000 to 2999999 and may be used to link the ED stay with the hospitalization in MIMIC-IV. If hadm_id is NULL, the patient was not admitted to the hospital after their ED stay. The intime and outtime columns are the admission and discharge datetimes of the emergency department stay.

The arrival_transport column of the edstays table records the method through which the individual arrived at the ED. The entries are WALK IN (251849 stays), AMBULANCE (155752), UNKNOWN (15352), OTHER (1266) and HELICOPTER (868). The disposition column records the method through which the individual left the ED.
